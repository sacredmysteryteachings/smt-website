Privacy Policy

Who we are

Sacred Mystery Teachings is a tarot education business run by Christian in Utah, USA. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email sacredmysteryteachings@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Booking details: when you book a reading, your name, email address, the time you choose, and your answers to the booking questions, including the disclaimer checkbox.
  • Email list details: if you sign up for our emails, your name (if you give it) and email address, and basic information about which emails you open or click.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.
  • Payment details: when paid readings begin, your payment is handled by a payment company through our booking tool. We see your name, the amount, and whether the payment went through. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Website visits: our website host automatically logs your IP address (the number that identifies your internet connection) for security.

What you share in a reading

What you tell us during a reading stays private. We don't record or transcribe readings. Christian may keep brief private notes to help with future readings. We never share what you say in a reading, except if the law requires it, or if we believe someone is in immediate danger.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, hold, and follow up on your readings.
  • To send you emails you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer your questions and keep our business and tax records.
  • To improve our readings and services.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Services we use

We use a few trusted services to run the business. They handle your information on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Calendly, for booking (and, later, payments). Calendly's booking pages may use their own cookies.
  • Google, for video readings (Google Meet) and email (Gmail).
  • An email list service, for our newsletter.
  • A payment company, such as Stripe or PayPal, when paid readings begin.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

Tracking and cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't follow your activity across other websites. Because we don't track you, we don't change anything when your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal. Other services we link to, such as Calendly's booking page, may use their own cookies and may collect information about your online activity over time and across sites, under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to provide our services and to meet legal, tax, and record-keeping duties. After that, we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you. Email sacredmysteryteachings@gmail.com, and we'll respond within 30 days. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time with the link in any email. Some records, such as payment records, may need to be kept for tax reasons.

Age

Our services are for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Where your information is stored

We're based in the United States, and the services we use store information in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date below. If the change is important, we'll also email people on our list.
