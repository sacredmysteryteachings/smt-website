Readings Disclaimer

What a reading is

A tarot reading with Sacred Mystery Teachings is a reflective, spiritual practice. The cards are used as a mirror: a way to look at where you feel stuck, what matters to you, and where you might grow. When we talk about "growth and healing," we mean personal and spiritual growth, in your own words and within your own beliefs. Each reading is Christian's interpretation of the cards. What you take from it is up to you, and no particular result is promised.

What a reading is not

A reading is not medical, mental health, legal, financial, or other professional advice, and it is not diagnosis or treatment of any condition. Christian is not a licensed therapist, counselor, doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor. A reading does not replace care from a qualified professional. Please don't stop, delay, or change any treatment, medication, or professional advice because of a reading.

No predictions

Readings don't claim to tell the future. When we explore possibilities, it's to help you reflect, not to say what will happen.

Your choices are your own

You are responsible for the decisions you make in your life, including any you make after a reading.

If you're in crisis

Readings aren't for emergencies. If you're thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you're in danger, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 right away. If a concern like this comes up during a reading, Christian may pause or end the reading and encourage you to reach out for professional support.
