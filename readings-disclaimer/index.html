Readings Disclaimer

What a reading is

A tarot reading with Sacred Mystery Teachings LLC is a reflective, spiritual practice. I'm Christian, and I give the readings. In this disclaimer, "I," "me" and "my" mean Sacred Mystery Teachings LLC and me. The cards are used as a mirror: a way to look at where you feel stuck, what matters to you, and where you might grow. When I talk about "growth and healing," I mean personal and spiritual growth, in your own words and within your own beliefs. Each reading is my interpretation of the cards. What you take from it is up to you, and no particular result is promised.

What a reading is not

A reading is not medical, mental health, legal, financial, or other professional advice, and it is not diagnosis or treatment of any condition. I am not a licensed therapist, counselor, doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor. A reading does not replace care from a qualified professional. Please don't stop, delay, or change any treatment, medication, or professional advice because of a reading.

No predictions

Readings don't claim to tell the future. When I explore possibilities with you, it's to help you reflect, not to say what will happen.

Your choices are your own

You are responsible for the decisions you make in your life, including any you make after a reading.

If you're in crisis

Readings aren't for emergencies. If you're thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you're in danger, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 right away. If a concern like this comes up during a reading, I may pause or end the reading and encourage you to reach out for professional support.

Limits of responsibility

I offer readings in good faith, but I can't promise any particular outcome. To the fullest extent the law allows, Sacred Mystery Teachings LLC and I aren't responsible for decisions you make or results you experience based on a reading. Nothing here limits any rights you have under Utah or federal law, or my responsibility for my own serious misconduct.
